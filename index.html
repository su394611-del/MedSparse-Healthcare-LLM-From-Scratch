01 / ENGINEERING OVERVIEW

Built to understand every layer.

Rather than loading official pretrained Mixtral weights, the project implements its own Mixtral-inspired decoder and trains it through two explicitly separated stages.

◈01 / ATTENTION

Grouped-query attention

Six query heads and two key/value heads, rotary position embeddings and causal scaled dot-product attention.

GQARoPESDPA
✳02 / MIXTURE-OF-EXPERTS

Top-2 sparse routing

Per-token routing across four independent SwiGLU experts with normalized weights and a balancing objective.

4 expertsTop-2Router loss
∑03 / MODEL TRAINING

Full-model optimization

AdamW updates embeddings, attention, expert weights, router and output head. No LoRA or frozen backbone.

AdamWRMSNormFull SFT
⌘04 / DATA PIPELINE

Healthcare-first datasets

Biomedical text for pretraining, followed by separate medical instruction data and completion-only supervision.

HF DatasetsBPEAnswer masks

02 / END-TO-END LIFECYCLE

One architecture. Two training stages.

The pretrained checkpoint becomes the starting point for medical supervised fine-tuning. The two model releases are separate artifacts with separate documentation.

00DATA & TOKENIZER

Audit, tokenize, pack

Screen approved healthcare corpora, train a biomedical BPE tokenizer, and form causal language-model training sequences.

InputBiomedical text
OutputTokenizer + token IDs
01FROM-SCRATCH PRETRAINING

Optimize the native MoE Transformer with next-token cross-entropy and an auxiliary expert-load balancing term.

Engineering scope and evaluation limitations
The documented training configuration contains approximately 38.8 million parameters and is distinct from larger reference presets also included in the repository. The model is randomly initialized for Stage 1; Stage 2 initializes from its native checkpoint with all parameters trainable. The examples use an end-to-end sequence length of 128 tokens, not a verified million-token context.


Cross-entropy and perplexity help assess language-model training, but do not establish medical accuracy, clinical suitability or safety. Training targets are not presented as proof of completed runs. Dataset licensing, provenance, patient privacy, and expert-reviewed medical evaluation remain required.

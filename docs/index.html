01 / MODEL ARCHITECTURE

A sparse Transformer, built natively.

The model does not download pretrained Mixtral or Mistral parameters. It constructs its own decoder stack, learns healthcare language representations from random initialization, and retains the same architecture for medical SFT.

∑

Sparse Mixture-of-Experts

Token-level router selects two of four SwiGLU experts. An auxiliary load-balancing term discourages routing collapse.

◎

Grouped-query attention

Six query heads share two key/value heads with rotary positional embeddings and causal scaled-dot-product attention.

⌁

Pre-normalized decoder

Four residual Transformer blocks combine RMSNorm, attention and sparse expert feed-forward computation.

↻

Full parameter optimization

Embeddings, attention projections, expert networks, routing weights and output head remain trainable during both stages.

▣

Native checkpoints

PyTorch state dictionaries support model and optimizer restoration. Stage 2 starts from pretrained weights with a fresh optimizer.

⊞

Config-driven research

Architectural sizes and attention modes live in JSON presets. Extended-context presets are experimental, not validated capabilities.

02 / END-TO-END TRAINING

From raw biomedical text to medical SFT.

A two-stage training lifecycle with separate objectives, controlled dataset sources and explicit checkpoint lineage. The step counts below are configured targets, not automatically verified completion claims.

STAGE 00

Corpus audit & tokenizer

Review dataset provenance and licenses; stream biomedical material and train a healthcare-domain BPE tokenizer.

PubMed · Hugging Face
STAGE 01

Pretrain from scratch

Randomly initialize all model weights. Optimize next-token cross-entropy plus auxiliary expert load-balancing loss.

5,000 optimizer steps · target
STAGE 02

Full medical SFT

Initialize from the Stage 1 checkpoint. Supervise medical answers while masking the system and user prompt tokens.

2,000 optimizer steps · target
STAGE 03

Evaluate & release

Track held-out completion loss and perplexity, export model-only weights, and document limitations in model cards.

Research and clinical-use boundaries

This is an engineering and research project. Cross-entropy and perplexity measure language-model development, not medical correctness. The models must not be used to diagnose, prescribe, triage patients or guide medical treatment without suitable independent clinical and safety validation.

Reproducibility and configuration clarity

The documented experiment trains with seq_len=128; a larger max_seq_len field does not establish long-context quality. Parameter counts depend on the selected JSON configuration. Public model pages and cards should report the actual checkpoint step and evaluation evidence.
